The problem

The defects that cost the most are the ones you can't see.

A cell can look perfect from the outside and still carry a fault that shows up as scrap, a warranty claim or a safety incident.

  1. 01

    Sampling misses thingsChecking a few cells per batch leaves most of the line uninspected, so hidden defects ship.

  2. 02

    Teardown destroys the evidenceDestructive analysis tells you about the cell you cut open, not the thousands you didn't.

  3. 03

    End-of-line is too lateBy formation and final test, the value has already been added to a cell that may be scrapped.

How it works

From echoes to insight

Sound travels differently through electrode, separator, electrolyte and gas. Celmetrix reads those differences and turns them into a decision.

01 02 03
01 · SENSE

Proprietary transducers

Custom ultrasound sensors and electronics send sound through the cell at multiple points without touching its chemistry.

02 · ANALYSE

Edge analytics

Signal processing and classification models run at the edge, mapping each echo to fill, wetting, gas and structure.

03 · ACT

Fitness score + diagnostics

Each cell gets an actionable fitness score and detailed diagnostics, delivered through an API into your MES and line control.

Applications

Real-time quality control, from production to end-of-life

How Celmetrix finds defects early across the battery supply chain. Our standalone node has been validated at UKBIC through the Faraday Battery Challenge.

01

Filling stage

Electrolyte fill monitoring

Challenge

Inconsistencies at the fill stage affect cell performance, safety and lifespan, and usually aren't visible until much later.

Approach

Inline monitoring with edge analytics assesses fill quality in real time, cell by cell.

Outcome

Early detection of deviations, tighter process control, less waste and better quality and yield.

02

Formation and ageing

Early gas detection

Challenge

Gas build-up can signal internal defects, safety risks or manufacturing problems.

Approach

Multi-point inline sensing plus edge analytics identifies gas-related anomalies in real time.

Outcome

Improved safety, less rework, and only high-quality cells move on through production.

03

Filling and wetting

Fill level detection

Challenge

The wrong electrolyte volume reduces ionic conductivity, limiting capacity, power and fast-charge performance, and is linked to faster ageing, gassing, impedance rise and early capacity fade.

Approach

Analysis and classification algorithms turn ultrasound signals into point clusters that separate good cells from the rest.

Outcome

Underfilled, overfilled and poorly wetted cells are flagged inline, before they're finished.

Space reserved for pilot results (yield change, scrap avoided, detection rate) once partners approve publication.

Advancing Battery Intelligence at Celmetrix: Q3 2026 Update

Celmetrix
Birmingham, UK
Q3 2026From echoes to insight

Celmetrix is building ultrasound-based, non-destructive diagnostics for battery research and manufacturing quality assurance. From June to September 2026, we progressed sensor validation, explored new applications and connected with organisations working across the battery lifecycle supply chain. These activities share a clear purpose: to develop practical inspection technologies that help industry assess battery quality, improve manufacturing processes and make more informed decisions about reuse.

Connecting across the battery ecosystem

In June, we joined the Cross-Sector Battery Showcase in Leicester, bringing our inspection technology into conversations spanning automotive, aerospace, rail, maritime and defence.

July brought Celmetrix to the Battery Cells & Systems Expo at the NEC Birmingham, alongside the co-located Vehicle Electrification Expo, Advanced Materials Show and Advanced Ceramics Show. Exhibiting in the Scale-Up Pavilion gave us the opportunity to discuss real inspection challenges with cell developers, manufacturers, testing centres and research teams. Our CEO, Dr. Nishal Ramadas, also presented “From Echoes to Insight: Ultrasound for Battery Quality and Industrial Intelligence” on the Scale-Up Accelerator Stage. The presentation explored the potential of ultrasound to reveal internal cell behaviour and provide useful information throughout battery development, manufacturing and subsequent assessment. Our focus is on translating these measurements into insights that help engineers understand variation, investigate quality concerns and plan the next step with greater confidence.

Our engagement also extended into the wider energy and research community.

  1. At the Energy Capital Partnership Autumn Conference in Birmingham, we connected with organisations supporting the West Midlands energy transition and explored how battery diagnostics could contribute to regional innovation and decarbonisation priorities.
  2. The UK Knowledge Exchange Hub for Mathematical Sciences opened further conversations around industrial challenges and research collaboration. These connections support our ambition to combine advanced sensing with mathematics, modelling and data science, making diagnostic measurements more useful to the people developing and manufacturing batteries.

A clearer identity for the next phase

September also marked the evolution of our brand from Hy-Met to Celmetrix, giving our work a clearer identity centred on battery quality and industrial intelligence. Our next phase will focus on validating performance, developing demonstrators with partners and preparing technologies for industrial use. We see opportunities across manufacturing quality assurance, battery development and second-life assessment.

We welcome conversations with battery manufacturers, research organisations, equipment suppliers and recycling businesses interested in developing and validating these applications with us. Get in touch with us to discuss your inspection challenges and opportunities for collaboration.

A Week of Reflection, Inspiration, and Collaboration

Nishal Ramadas
Birmingham, UK

Last week has been one of mixed emotions for me, a time to remember, to contribute, and to look ahead. It's been a week that reminded me how much we can achieve when passion, purpose, and partnership come together, and how important it is to carry forward the legacy of those who helped us get here.

Remembering John: A Foundational Force

We are deeply saddened to share the passing of dear friend and colleague John, one of the key members of our team. As the first full-time employee of our parent company, he played a pivotal role in establishing several early collaborations and laying the foundation for our battery technology products. His energy, dedication and belief in what we were building will always remain a part of our story.

To honour his legacy, we plan to sponsor a research studentship in his name from 2026, ensuring that his spirit of curiosity and innovation continues to inspire future generations.

Mentoring the Next Generation

Earlier in the week, I had the opportunity to mentor a group of students during the Hackathon at STEAMhouse. It was fantastic to see the creativity and enthusiasm of young innovators tackling real-world challenges, a reminder of the power of collaborative problem-solving and the value of nurturing emerging talent. These moments of connection with the next generation of innovators are precious. Watching students approach complex problems with fresh perspectives and unbridled enthusiasm reinforces why we do what we do.

Celebrating Regional Excellence

Midweek brought the Make UK Regional Awards dinner night for the Midlands, and I'd like to thank WMG for the kind invitation to join. It was truly inspiring to witness the incredible work that local manufacturing businesses and their apprentices are doing to drive innovation and growth across the region. It was also wonderful to reconnect with former colleagues and long-time industry partners. These relationships, built over years of collaboration and shared challenges, form the backbone of our regional innovation ecosystem.

Building Partnerships for the Future

The week concluded with contributing to the co-creation meeting with West Midlands Combined Authority (WMCA) on the Local Innovation Partnership Fund (LIPF) proposal, a promising step towards aligning regional innovation efforts to create meaningful, long-term impact for the West Midlands and beyond. These partnerships are crucial for translating innovative ideas into real-world solutions that benefit our communities. By working together across sectors and organisations, we can leverage collective expertise and resources to tackle the challenges that matter most, and we cannot wait to contribute towards making this initiative a success.

Reflections on a Meaningful Week

This week has reinforced several important lessons about leadership, collaboration, and the interconnected nature of our work:

  • Legacy matters: The people who help build our organisations leave lasting impacts that extend far beyond their time with us.
  • Investment in youth: Mentoring and supporting emerging talent is not just about giving back; it's about ensuring the continuity of innovation.
  • Community strength: Regional networks and partnerships create resilience and shared prosperity.
  • Collaborative impact: The most meaningful progress happens when we work together toward common goals.

As leaders in our respective fields, we have a responsibility to honour those who came before us, support those coming up behind us, and work collaboratively to build a better future for our communities. The convergence of remembrance, mentorship, celebration, and forward-planning in a single week feels somehow fitting: a reminder that leadership is about maintaining connections across time and space, bridging the past with the future through purposeful action in the present.

Nishal Ramadas, 10 November 2025, Birmingham UK

Cenex Expo 2025: Reflections from Celmetrix

Celmetrix
Birmingham, UK
Cenex Expo 2025Innovate UK stand C3-309

This year’s Cenex Expo 2025 was a milestone moment for Celmetrix. As part of the Innovate UK stand (C3-309), we were able to showcase our vision for sustainable battery innovation and connect with some of the most forward-thinking minds in clean technology and mobility.

Events like Cenex Expo remind us why collaboration is so important: to accelerate progress, encourage knowledge-sharing, and ensure a cleaner, smarter, and more resilient transport future.

We are excited about what lies ahead and grateful to everyone who took the time to meet with us. The conversations we had will continue to shape our journey in the months to come.
